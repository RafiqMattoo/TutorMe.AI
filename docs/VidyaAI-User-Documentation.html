Overview

About VidyaAI

VidyaAI is an enterprise-grade, NEP 2020-aligned School Management System for Indian schools. It brings the full life of a school — admissions and student records, academic structure, transport, AI-assisted teaching content, and parent communication — into one secure, multi-school platform.

This document is a guide for the people who use VidyaAI every day: Super Administrators who run the platform, School Administrators who manage a school, Teachers who create and deliver learning, and Students & Parents who learn and follow progress. Each chapter explains, in plain language, what you can do and how the common workflows fit together.

How to read this guide

Every module lists who uses it and the actions available. Look for the blue chips for the audience, and the numbered flows for step-by-step workflows such as onboarding a school or delivering a lesson.

Chapter 1

Roles & Who Can Do What

VidyaAI is multi-tenant: many schools share one platform, but each school's data stays private. What you see and can do depends on your role.

Super Administrator

Runs the whole platform. Sees every school and all content. Creates and approves schools, manages subscriptions, and can act across the entire system.

School Administrator

Manages a single school. Approves teachers and students, builds the academic structure, manages student records and transport, and oversees all content for the school.

Teacher

The content and teaching workspace. Uploads materials, generates AI flashcards/quizzes/lesson plans, writes articles, and schedules deliveries to students.

Student

The learning view. Consumes today's deliveries, reads materials and articles, studies flashcards, takes quizzes, and asks the AI Tutor questions.

Parent

The guardian view. Follows school content and a child's learning activity, and listens to narrated lessons.

Scope rule

The Super Administrator works globally. Everyone else is scoped to their own school — they never see another school's students, content, or data.

Chapter 2

Getting Started: Registration & Sign-in

Used by: Everyone

Signing in

Go to the Login page and enter your email and password. VidyaAI keeps you signed in securely and renews your session automatically, so you are not logged out mid-task. If your account is still pending, rejected, or deactivated, you'll see a clear message explaining why.

Registering a new school

A school can sign itself up from the Register page — providing the school details and the first administrator's account. The school starts in a Pending state and becomes active only after a Super Administrator approves it.

  1. School fills the registration form (school details + admin account).
  2. The school and its admin are created in Pending.
  3. A Super Administrator reviews it under Approvals.
  4. On approval: the school is activated, a trial period begins, and the admin is notified that they can now sign in.

Registering as a teacher or student

Teachers and students can self-register and pick their school. They land in a Pending queue until the School Administrator approves them.

Passwords must include at least one uppercase letter and one digit. Accounts created directly by an administrator are approved automatically and skip the queue.

Chapter 3

Dashboard & Analytics

Used by: Everyone (figures adapt to your role)

The Dashboard is your landing page after sign-in. It summarises activity at a glance and adapts to your scope — a Super Administrator sees platform-wide totals, while a School Administrator sees only their own school.

What you'll see

  • Key counts — schools, total users, students and teachers, and articles (published vs. draft).
  • Engagement — comments, likes, and total views across content.
  • Top articles — the five most-viewed published articles with author and stats.
  • Recent activity — the ten latest articles and drafts with status and timestamp.
  • This week — new users and new articles added in the last seven days.
Chapter 4

School Management

Used by: Super Administrator

The Schools area is where the Super Administrator manages every school on the platform.

What you can do

  • Browse schools — a searchable, paginated list (by name or city) showing subscription plan, status, user count, and article count.
  • View a school — full profile with address, board type (CBSE, ICSE, etc.), and subscription details.
  • Create a school — added directly by the Super Administrator; it is auto-approved and starts active.
  • Edit a school — update name, address, contact, logo, and activation status.
  • Manage subscription — set the plan (Free / Premium / Enterprise), status (Trial / Active / Expired), and expiry date.
  • Remove a school — soft-deleted so records are preserved for audit.
Chapter 5

Approvals Workflow

Used by: Super Administrator (schools) · School Administrator (members)

Approvals is the gatekeeping queue. Nothing self-registered goes live until it's reviewed here.

Pending schools — Super Administrator

  • Approve — activates the school, its admin, and all enrollments; starts a 14-day trial if no expiry is set; notifies the admin.
  • Reject — rejects the school and admin, suspends enrollments, and notifies the admin.

Pending members — School Administrator

Teachers and students awaiting approval. A School Administrator sees only their own school's pending members; the Super Administrator sees all.

  • Approve member — activates the account and notifies the user.
  • Reject member — deactivates the account and notifies the user.
Chapter 6

Users, Roles & Enrollments

Used by: Super Administrator (global) · School Administrator (their school)

Users

  • Browse — searchable, paginated list by email or name, filterable by school; shows role, status, email-verified, and last login.
  • Create — the Super Administrator can create any user; a School Administrator can create teachers and students. Admin-created users are auto-approved.
  • Student fields — grade level, roll number, date of birth, and guardian name/phone can be captured at creation.
  • Enable / disable and remove (soft delete) accounts.

Roles

A reference view of the role hierarchy and each role's scope — Super Administrator, School Administrator, Teacher, Student, and Parent — as described in Chapter 1.

Enrollments

Enrollments connect a user to a school with a specific role and status. A single user can belong to more than one school.

  • View enrollments with role and status (Active / Pending / Suspended).
  • Create an enrollment to add an existing user to a school.
  • Set primary — mark one enrollment as the user's main school.
  • Remove a user from a school.
Chapter 7

Academic Structure

Used by: School Administrator · Teacher

This is where a school's academic backbone is built, fully aligned to NEP 2020. The page is organised into tabs.

Classes

Create classes (grades 0–12+), assign the NEP stage (Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, Secondary), and set the level used for promotion.

Sections

Divide a class into sections (e.g. 5-A, 5-B), assign a class teacher, set capacity, and link to a stream where relevant.

Subjects

Define subjects with code and medium of instruction. Flag a subject as a language (three-language formula) or as co-scholastic (arts, sports, life-skills for the Holistic Progress Card).

Subject–Teacher Allocations

Map each subject to a class/section and the assigned teacher. This drives timetabling, assessment, and report cards.

Streams

Create academic tracks (Science, Commerce, Humanities) and link them to sections at the Secondary stage.

Houses

Set up school houses for points and identity — name, optional house master, and a colour.

Grading Scales

Configure grade scales by board (e.g. CBSE 9-point), set a default for the school, and define grade bands (A1, A2, …) with percentage ranges and grade points.

Academic Years & Terms

Create the session (e.g. 2025–26) with start/end dates, mark exactly one as current, and add nested terms or semesters that drive the exam and report-card calendar.

Chapter 8

Student Information System (SIS)

Used by: School Administrator & Teacher (view) · School Administrator (create/edit)

The SIS is the master register of every student in the school.

Finding students

Search the paginated list by name, admission number, or roll number, and filter by class, section, or status (Active, Inactive, Transferred-Out, Graduated, Alumni). Each row opens a full detail view.

Adding & editing a student

  • Identity — name, gender, date of birth, admission and roll numbers, photo.
  • Placement — academic year, class, section, and house.
  • Contact — email, phone, and full address.
  • Demographics & compliance — category (General/SC/ST/OBC), blood group, nationality, mother tongue, religion; CWSN (special needs) flag, RTE (EWS quota) flag, and Aadhaar / APAAR ID for one-nation-one-student records.
  • Guardians — father/mother and guardian details with phone, occupation, and relation.
  • Account link — optionally connect the student to a Student or Parent login.
Life cycle

A student record progresses through its life cycle: Active → Graduated / Transferred-Out → Alumni / Inactive, keeping a complete history.

Chapter 9

Transport Management

Used by: School Administrator

Vehicles

Register buses and vans with registration number, model, and capacity; capture driver name and phone; and activate or deactivate each vehicle.

Routes

Create routes with name, code, and description; assign a vehicle; and set the fare and frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or Per-Trip). Each route shows its stop count and how many students are allocated.

Stops

Define ordered stops along a route with pickup and drop times and an optional stop-specific fare.

Student Allocation

Allocate students to a route and stop, capturing the fare at allocation time. Allocations can be activated or deactivated to handle changes and absences.

Chapter 10

Content: Articles, Categories & Materials

Articles

Create: Super Admin · School Admin · Teacher   |   Read: Everyone
  • Write a draft with title, rich-text body, optional cover image, and a YouTube video link.
  • Organise with a category and comma-separated tags.
  • Publish now, schedule for a future time, or unpublish back to draft.
  • Engagement — readers can view, like, and comment (with nested replies and comment likes).

Categories

Used by: School Administrator · Teacher

Create, edit, and delete categories to organise articles and materials, with sort order, an optional icon, and an active/inactive flag.

Materials (PDF documents)

Upload: Super Admin · School Admin · Teacher   |   Read: Everyone

Upload a PDF and VidyaAI processes it in the background so it can power the AI tools. You'll see the indexing status move from Processing to Ready (or Failed with a reason).

  • Metadata — title, file name, size, and page count.
  • Categorise into a content category.
  • Behind the scenes the PDF is split into sections and indexed so the Tutor, flashcards, quizzes, and lesson plans can draw on it.
Chapter 11

AI Study Tools

Generate: Super Admin · School Admin · Teacher   |   Use: Everyone

From any Ready material, teachers can generate study resources with AI in a few clicks.

Flashcards

  • Generate a set of 3–30 cards from a material, each with a question on the front and a detailed answer on the back.
  • Study mode lets learners flip through and review cards.

Quizzes

  • Generate 3–25 multiple-choice questions at Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty, each with four options and an explanation.
  • Students take the quiz and their score and answers are recorded as an attempt.

Lesson Plans

  • Generate a structured plan from a material by giving the subject, grade level, and duration (15–240 minutes).
  • The plan is formatted with objectives, activities, and assessment, and can be browsed and viewed by teachers.
Chapter 12

Teaching & Learning Tools

AI Tutor

Used by: Everyone

Ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in the school's uploaded materials. The Tutor finds the most relevant sections, answers with citations back to the source, and keeps multi-turn chat sessions so you can browse your history later.

Narration, Recite & Explain

Configure: Teacher   |   Listen / watch: Student · Parent

Any material can be turned into audio in two styles:

  • Recite (verbatim) — the exact text read aloud, with the words highlighted in sync as they're spoken.
  • Explain (simplified) — dense passages are simplified into an animated explainer with synchronised illustrations.

Teachers choose the voice; learners simply press play. Audio is prepared in the background and becomes available once ready.

Scenes

Used by: Everyone

A preview area reserved for upcoming visual, scenario-based learning.

Chapter 13

Deliveries & Today

Creating deliveries

Used by: Teacher · School Administrator

A delivery is a packaged learning task assigned to a calendar day. Give it a title and instructions, pick the date, and attach any combination of a material to read, a quiz to practise, and a flashcard set to study (at least one is required). Deliveries can be edited, filtered by date, or removed.

Today

Used by: Everyone

The Today page shows students and parents what has been delivered for the day, with one-tap buttons to read, listen, or practise — opening straight into the material viewer, quiz, or flashcard study mode.

A teacher's content-to-delivery flow

  1. Upload a PDF material and wait for it to become Ready.
  2. Generate flashcards, a quiz, and/or a lesson plan from it.
  3. Optionally publish an article or generate a narration.
  4. Create a delivery, attach the content, and schedule it for a date.
  5. Students see it on Today and start learning.
Chapter 14

Notifications

Used by: Everyone

VidyaAI keeps users informed about the things that matter to them, especially around approvals:

  • Approval requested — a new school registration is waiting for the Super Administrator.
  • Approval granted — a school or member has been approved and can sign in.
  • Approval rejected — a registration was declined.

Notifications are marked read or unread and link back to the related school or user.
